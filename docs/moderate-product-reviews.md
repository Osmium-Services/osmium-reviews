**Product Reviews** is where customer reviews of your products are managed. New reviews always wait for your approval before they show.

## Be told about new reviews

Press **Settings** and list the **Email addresses** that should get an email whenever a customer sends a review, one per line. Leave it empty to send none.

## Manage reviews

The **Review Management** list shows each review's product, reviewer, rating, text, status and when it was submitted.

- **Edit** opens the review. You can change the text, the rating and the status, and add photos (JPG, PNG or WebP). The reviewer's email is never shown on the site.
- **Unpublish** takes a published review off the site.
- **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Reviews**. **Purge All** there removes them for good.

## Add a review yourself

Press **Add Review**, choose the **Product**, enter the **Name** and **Review Text**, set the **Rating** and **Status**, and press save. The text is plain text: line breaks are kept and nothing typed there can run as code.
